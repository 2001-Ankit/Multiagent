As someone interested in AI, finance, and building products, I've been following the latest developments in the field. In this post, I'll share my findings on the recent trends in AI, including key facts, how they work, and why they matter.

## Introduction to Recent Trends
The recent trends in AI are focused on making the technology more accessible, reliable, and secure. According to a report by IBM, smaller models are gaining significant enterprise traction, delivering targeted capability at a fraction of the cost of large models. This is due to a collapse in inference cost, making it more economical to run smaller models.

## Key Trends in AI
Some of the key trends in AI include:
* **Agentic AI**: This type of AI allows systems to handle tasks autonomously, planning, invoking tools, checking results, and trying again when something fails.
* **Generative AI**: This type of AI generates internal chains of logic, verifies facts against trusted databases, and provides responses, reducing hallucinations and making the technology more reliable.
* **Reliability and Security**: With the increasing use of AI, reliability and security are becoming major focuses, especially in applications where mistakes can have significant consequences.
* **OpenClaw**: An early example of a technology that allows AI to take actions and make decisions, highlighting the need for reliability and security.

## Applications of Recent Trends
The recent trends in AI have various applications, including:
* **Chatbots and Autonomous Partners**: AI can now be used to summarize video meetings, identify speakers and key points, and even provide repair instructions for broken machines.
* **Content Creation and Search Rankings**: AI-powered search is changing the way content is created and ranked, with new research highlighting the importance of understanding how AI search rankings work.
* **Work Transformation**: AI is transforming the way we work, with new data from Microsoft 365 Copilot telemetry signals demonstrating AI transformation in action.

## Best Practices for Working with AI
When working with AI, it's essential to:
* **Learn enough to ask better questions**: Understand the limitations and potential biases of AI systems.
* **Protect sensitive information**: Verify high-stakes claims and keep building the human side of AI.
* **Stay up-to-date with the latest trends and developments**: Follow reputable sources and blogs to stay informed about the latest advancements in AI.
